Privacy Policy.

At a glance: Coin Strike has no player account or cloud wallet. Game progress is saved locally. Google Play processes optional purchases. Google Firebase services process app usage and technical diagnostics. This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

01. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Coin Strike Android application, package com.fallowmint.harvesttrolley, and this accompanying website. “We,” “us” and “our” refer to the developer of Coin Strike, who can be contacted at limoor.kastrk0@gmail.com.

Coin Strike is an entertainment game using virtual coins. It does not offer cash prizes, withdrawals or exchanges for real-world value. You do not need to create an account or provide your name or email address to play.

02. Information saved on your device

The app stores information needed to continue your game:

  • Your virtual coin balance, chosen bet, reel result, remaining free spins, triggering bet and any unfinished bonus from an earlier version.
  • Game statistics, level, symbol collection, missions and reward claim times.
  • Your most recent 100 game results.
  • Sound, haptic feedback, Turbo and welcome-screen preferences.
  • Product identifiers and hashed purchase-token records used to prevent duplicate coin delivery.

This game database is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not upload this database to a developer-operated cloud wallet. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for this app’s data. Separate diagnostic and analytics information is described below.

The current app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise GPS location.

03. Google Play purchases

Optional virtual coin purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. The app receives purchase information such as the product ID, purchase state, quantity and purchase token to deliver coins and finish processing the transaction.

A hash of the purchase token is retained locally with the product ID to prevent the same purchase from granting coins twice. The raw purchase token is handled during billing processing rather than stored in the game wallet. We do not receive your full payment-card number or your Google account password.

Google processes payment and transaction information under its own terms and Privacy Policy. Google Analytics can also record purchase events, as described in the next section. Consumed coin packs are tied to your local wallet and cannot be restored to that wallet after uninstalling or clearing app storage.

04. Analytics and technical diagnostics

The Android app integrates the following Google Firebase services to understand app usage, investigate failures and improve performance. Collection can depend on device settings and service configuration.

Google Analytics for Firebase

Analytics processes app-instance identifiers, app lifecycle and session events, screen views, and purchase events such as product information and price. It can collect the resettable Android advertising ID when available and derive approximate location from masked IP information. These data help us understand how the app is used. See Google’s Analytics data disclosure.

Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics processes crash stack traces, app state, device and operating-system metadata, installation identifiers, and diagnostic session information. Analytics events may be used as breadcrumbs to understand what happened before a crash.

Firebase Performance Monitoring

Performance Monitoring processes startup and rendering measurements, network timing and technical device information. It uses installation identifiers and IP information, including country-level context, to analyze app performance.

These diagnostics are sent to Google Firebase. See the Firebase Android data disclosures for the provider’s explanation of automatic SDK collection. The app has no in-app analytics or diagnostics opt-out switch in the current version.

05. Sharing, security and service providers

We use Google Play for billing and Google Firebase for the analytics and diagnostics described above. Their infrastructure providers may process information to operate those services. Data may be processed outside your country in accordance with the providers’ applicable terms and safeguards.

We do not sell your personal information. Information you send to support is used to address your request and may be shared with a relevant service provider when needed to investigate it. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to address fraud, security incidents or misuse.

The local wallet uses Android app-private storage. Firebase sends collected data over encrypted connections. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Learn more in Firebase Privacy and Security and Google’s Privacy Policy.

06. Retention and deletion

Local game data: retained while the app’s storage remains on your device. You can delete it using Android Settings → Apps → Coin Strike → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app. Names of these settings may vary by device. This permanently removes the local wallet, collected items, preferences and game history.

Diagnostics: Google describes a 90-day retention period for Crashlytics crash information and associated identifiers before removal begins. Performance Monitoring retains IP-associated events for 30 days, and installation-associated or de-identified performance data for 60 days before removal begins. See the provider’s retention information.

Analytics: retention depends on the Analytics property settings and applicable Google service rules. Contact us for information about the configuration applying to your request. Clearing local storage does not automatically erase analytics or diagnostic information already sent to Google.

Payments and support: Google may retain transaction records under its own policies and legal obligations. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

07. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose whether to make purchases, manage relevant advertising-identifier settings through Android, and delete local game data as described above. Device advertising settings do not necessarily disable all analytics or diagnostics.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain processing, restrict processing or raise a complaint with a data protection authority. To submit a privacy request, email limoor.kastrk0@gmail.com.

Describe your request and the app version you use. Because there is no player account, we may need additional information to locate provider-held data and may not be able to associate every diagnostic record with a particular person. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

08. This website

This is a static website with locally hosted images, styles and scripts. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising pixels, contact forms, cookies or browser-storage identifiers to this website.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical information associated with requests, which may include IP addresses, browser or device information and request logs, to operate and protect its service. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google Play and other external links take you to services with their own privacy practices. Email links open your email application; information is sent to us only if you choose to send a message.

09. Children

Coin Strike is intended for adults and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request or our services, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps.

10. Contact and policy updates

We may update this policy when app features, service providers or data practices change. The updated policy will appear on this page with a revised date. Any additional notice or consent required by applicable law will be handled separately.
