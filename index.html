GOOD TO KNOW

A few things,
before you spin.

Need a hand? Get in touch ↗

Can I win or withdraw real money?

No. Coin Strike uses virtual coins for entertainment. Coins have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money or real-world prizes.

Do I have to buy coins?

No. You receive 1,000 starting coins and can collect free rewards. Additional virtual coin packs are optional purchases through Google Play. Buying coins never improves the odds.

Where is my progress saved?

Your wallet, settings and game progress are stored on your device. There is no account or cloud sync. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes this progress, including consumed coin packs.

Can I change the pace?

Yes. Choose normal or Turbo animation, or start a 10, 25 or 50-spin autoplay session. You can stop autoplay; it also stops on low balance, a bonus or leaving the game. Animation speed does not change the odds.

How does the app use data?

Gameplay is saved locally. Google Play handles payments, and Firebase services provide usage analytics, crash reports and performance diagnostics. Read our Privacy Policy for details and your options.
