Read the regular paytable

Your line bet is one tenth of the total bet. Matches run consecutively from the leftmost reel. The values below multiply the line bet.

Strike Coins pay as scatters and trigger free spins; they do not pay as regular symbols on a line.

Calculate a winning line

Example: a 1-coin total bet gives a 0.10-coin line bet. Three Lucky Sevens pay 113 × 0.10 = 11.30 virtual coins on a winning line. Five Lucky Sevens pay 565 × 0.10 = 56.50 virtual coins.

The highest eligible award on each line is paid. Wins on different lines are added together, along with any scatter award. These examples describe payout arithmetic, not the chance of a win.

Use WILD substitutions

Wild Strike can replace any regular fruit, bell, BAR or seven. It does not replace Strike Coins. A line made entirely of WILD symbols pays only when all five positions are WILD; three or four WILDs alone have no separate WILD award.

A prefix of WILD symbols may still complete a winning regular-symbol combination. For example, WILD, WILD, lemon, lemon, lemon qualifies as five lemons.

Trace the ten paylines

Read these row sequences from reel 1 to reel 5. T means top, M means middle and B means bottom.

  1. M – M – M – M – M
  2. T – T – T – T – T
  3. B – B – B – B – B
  4. T – M – B – M – T
  5. B – M – T – M – B
  6. T – T – M – B – B
  7. B – B – M – T – T
  8. M – T – T – T – M
  9. M – B – B – B – M
  10. T – M – M – M – T

All ten are active on every spin. The controls guide explains bets, Turbo and autoplay.
