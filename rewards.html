Build a seven-day streak

Claim your daily reward on consecutive days to move through the seven-day cycle. The rewards are 25, 50, 75, 100, 125, 150 and 175 virtual coins, for a total of 700 across a complete cycle.

Reward days follow UTC, changing at 00:00 UTC rather than your local midnight. You can claim once per UTC day. After day seven, the next consecutive claim starts a new cycle at day one. Missing a day also restarts the streak at day one.

Collect the four-hour gift

A free gift adds 25 virtual coins. After you claim it, the next gift becomes available four hours later. This timer is separate from the daily streak, so claiming one does not replace the other.

Rewards must be claimed in the app. A timer reaching zero does not automatically deposit a gift, and waiting longer does not stack several gifts into one claim. A new local wallet also begins with 1,000 virtual coins.

Keep track in Strike Club

Strike Club brings together your level, statistics and symbol collection. Discover the eleven symbols through play and track progress toward the collection mission. These milestones describe your progress; they do not increase the chance of winning.

Your reward times, claimed missions and collection are saved locally with the wallet. They do not sync between phones. See how local progress works before clearing storage. Optional packs are available through Google Play, but a purchase is not required to collect these rewards.
