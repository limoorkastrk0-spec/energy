Start with your virtual wallet

A new Coin Strike wallet starts with 1,000 virtual coins. Choose a total bet of 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 or 50 coins using the minus and plus controls. Tap Spin when you have enough coins for the selected bet.

The reels move continuously and stop one after another. Paid spins deduct the total bet; any line and scatter awards are then credited to the wallet. All amounts are virtual coins with no cash value.

Follow ten fixed paylines

All ten lines are active on every spin. A regular winning combination begins on the leftmost reel and continues for three, four or five matching symbols. WILD substitutes for regular symbols, but never for Strike Coins.

The total bet is split across ten lines. See the complete paytable for values and a worked example. Strike Coins pay separately and can trigger free spins anywhere on the board.

Set your pace

Turbo shortens the animation without changing the result probabilities. Hold the main Spin button to activate Turbo, or use its dedicated control. Autoplay offers 10, 25 or 50 spins and can be stopped manually.

Autoplay also stops on insufficient balance for a paid spin, a new bonus award or leaving the game. During free spins, the triggering bet stays fixed. Free spins do not deduct a new stake.

Make the club yours

Open Rewards for daily claims and missions, Shop for optional coin packs, History for the latest 100 rounds, and Club for your level, collection and sound, haptic and Turbo settings. The app supports portrait and landscape play.

Your wallet and unfinished bonus are stored on this device. No account or cloud sync is required. Read the progress FAQ before reinstalling or clearing storage.
