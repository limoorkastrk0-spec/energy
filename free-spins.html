Land three Strike Coins

Three or more Strike Coins anywhere in the five-by-three grid award free spins. They do not need to sit on a payline. Three coins give five free spins, four give seven and five give nine. Every additional Strike Coin adds two more free spins.

WILD does not substitute for a Strike Coin. A spin may have ordinary line wins and a Strike Coin award at the same time. Read the regular symbol rules for line combinations.

Calculate the scatter award

The scatter award is the number of Strike Coins minus two, multiplied by the total bet. Three coins pay 1× total bet, four pay 2× and five pay 3×. This amount is added to any winning paylines.

Example: at a 2-coin total bet, four Strike Coins award 4 virtual coins plus 7 free spins. The coin award is credited for the triggering round.

Use and retrigger free spins

Free spins use the bet that triggered the bonus, and no new bet is deducted. Each completed free spin uses one from your saved counter. Landing three or more Strike Coins during a free spin adds another award of free spins to the remaining total.

You cannot change the bet while free spins remain. Turbo only changes the reel animation. Buying coins does not improve the chance of triggering or extending a bonus.

Continue after a break

The remaining free spins, triggering bet and wallet are saved together before the reel animation begins. Returning to the app continues from the saved state on this device.

If this app was updated with an unfinished locked-coin bonus from an earlier version, that saved bonus remains playable under its original rules until it finishes. New spins then use the Coin Strike free-spin rules. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress.
